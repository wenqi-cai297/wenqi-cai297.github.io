Research Experience

  • 2026.09 - Present
    Self-Supervised Visual Representation Learning
    Exploring the Geometry of Visual Representation Spaces
    • Reproducing DINOv3 training on ViT-S/16 and ViT-L/16 and analyzing training trajectories to investigate the root causes of dense feature degradation and structural collapse of patch representations.
    • Investigating how relationships among patch representations evolve during training and which geometric structures in the representation space better support self-supervised learning and downstream dense prediction.
  • 2026.04 - 2026.08
    Human–Object Interaction Generation
    ULIFlow: Unified Latent Human-Object Interaction Flow
    • Proposed ULIFlow for text-conditioned 3D human–object interaction generation, treating continuous dynamic human–object relations as an explicit modality and encoding heterogeneous streams with a query-based Mixture-of-Transformers autoencoder.
    • Adapted Diffusion Forcing to block-wise Flow Forcing with progress-aware conditioning, enabling variable-length sequence generation from fixed-length training windows.
    • Generated coherent human and object motions jointly without heuristic post-refinement, supporting user-specified durations beyond the training horizon; first-author manuscript under review at ICLR 2027.
  • 2025.04 - 2026.02
    Generative Dataset Distillation
    EVLF: Early Vision-Language Fusion for Generative Dataset Distillation
    • Proposed EVLF, an early vision–language fusion framework that integrates VAE image latents and class-level text embeddings before diffusion denoising.
    • Designed a lightweight cross-attention module trained with InfoNCE semantic alignment and an MSE latent-preservation loss to retain visual structure while incorporating semantic guidance.
    • Generated semantically faithful and visually coherent distilled datasets, with consistent gains in downstream classification accuracy across diverse datasets and model architectures; published as first author at CVPR 2026.
  • 2024.06 - 2025.02
    Neural Network Explainability
    Improving the Explainability of Neural Network by Downstream Post-Optimization
    • Proposed a downstream post-optimization method to address co-occurrence bias in visual explanations of pretrained neural networks.
    • Applied Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to search fully connected layer weight configurations, selecting candidates using deletion and insertion metrics.
    • Reduced attention to irrelevant backgrounds and emphasized foreground features, improving explanation reliability and interpretability; published as first author at QCAV 2025.
